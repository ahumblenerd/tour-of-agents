**Who ranks:** Mirascope, Akka, IBM, Gumloop, Zapier, Apify, Lindy, Vellum, Lamatic, Lunary

**Action:** Write a blog post: "You Don't Need a LangChain Alternative — You Need 60 Lines of Python." Target this exact gap.

### "LangChain vs CrewAI vs AutoGen"
**Who ranks:** OpenAgents, o-mega.ai, Arsum, DataCamp, Apify, instinctools, SparkCo, BSWEN, Turing, Propelius
**Gap:** All comparison articles compare frameworks to each other. None compare to "no framework at all." Our /compare pages are the only ones that say "here's what it looks like in plain Python."
**Action:** Our compare pages are well-positioned but need more content depth to compete (most ranking pages are 2000+ words).

### "What is an AI agent"
**Who ranks:** Google Cloud, McKinsey, IBM, AWS, MIT Sloan, Wikipedia, Zapier, BCG, Datadog, Salesforce
**Gap:** These are all enterprise/cloud vendor pages. Very corporate. No code. Our angle: "Here's what an AI agent actually is — in code, not PowerPoint."
**Action:** This is a massive keyword but dominated by enterprise brands. Better to target long-tail: "how AI agents work in Python", "AI agent code example."

### "ReAct pattern" / "agent loop"
**Who ranks:** Daily Dose of DS, Simon Willison, Peter Roelants, LangChain docs, Medium posts
**Gap:** Simon Willison's TIL is the gold standard for simple explanations. Our Lesson 3 covers the same ground but is interactive.
